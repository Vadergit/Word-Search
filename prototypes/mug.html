6 × 6 curved grid

This version follows the reference mug appearance. The object rotates around its own true centre, all 36 tiles are visibly separated, selected tiles use the same mint/green highlight language as the other prototypes, and the mug is filled with coffee.

Controls

Rotate: drag empty space like direct touch manipulation on a fixed 3D object. The timer pauses during rotation.

Select: the 6×6 grid uses the Cube's eight-neighbour rule, including diagonals.

Trail: selected tiles turn mint and the rounded trail stays attached to the curved mug surface.
