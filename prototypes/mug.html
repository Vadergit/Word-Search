6 × 6 curved grid

The cup is a complete object rather than a flat board with a handle. Each playable tile is a curved front patch, and the Cube-style path follows the same cylinder radius between selected letters.

Controls

Rotate: drag empty space around the mug. The timer pauses during rotation.

Select: the 6×6 grid uses the Cube's eight-neighbour rule, including diagonals.

Trail: selected tiles turn mint and the rounded trail stays attached to the curved mug surface.
