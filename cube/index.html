Controls

Rotate: drag empty space beside the cube with mouse or touch. Rotation is unlimited in every direction; the word timer pauses while rotating.

Select: tap touching visible tiles. Diagonal steps are allowed on a face and across a cube edge. Tap any selected tile again to deselect it and safely shorten the path.

Difficulty: Beginner shows targets + hints, Middle hides targets but keeps hints, Hard hides both and uses more complex routes.

Time: the solve clock starts when the cube appears and stops when the last target word is found. Rotating the cube is part of the solve time.

Finish: target words are recognised automatically. For bonus words, wait 8 seconds or use Check word.
